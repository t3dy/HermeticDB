ALCHEMICAL

Chrysopoeia

ALCHEMICAL

The specific alchemical process of making gold from base metals.

...

Lapis Philosophorum

ALCHEMICAL

The ultimate goal of alchemy, capable of transmuting base metals into gold or granting immortality.

...

Prima Materia

ALCHEMICAL · ACTOR TERM

The original, undifferentiated substance required for the Great Work.

...

Quintessence

ALCHEMICAL

The fifth element; the ethereal substance beyond the four earthly elements.

...

Solve et Coagula

ALCHEMICAL

The fundamental alchemical operation of breaking down a substance and recombining it into a higher form.

...

Spagyrics

ALCHEMICAL

The alchemical practice of separating and reuniting plant or mineral components.

...

Tria Prima

ALCHEMICAL · ACTOR TERM

The three principles of matter: Sulfur, Salt, and Mercury.

...

COSMOLOGICAL

Anima Mundi

COSMOLOGICAL

The universal essence that connects all living things.

...

Decans

COSMOLOGICAL

36 ten-degree segments of the zodiac with specific spirits and influences.

...

Demiurge

COSMOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

The 'Craftsman' or creator god in Hermetic and Platonic thought. In the Poimandres, the Demiurge is the second Mind (Nous) who fashions the seven governors

...

Emanationism

COSMOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

The Neoplatonic model of creation overflowing from the One like light from the sun.

...

Heimarmene (Fate)

COSMOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

The concept of destiny or astronomical fate in Hermeticism. In many treatises, the goal of the practitioner is to rise above the Heimarmene through Gnosis.

...

Macrocosm and Microcosm

COSMOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

The structural mirror between the universe (macro) and the human body (micro).

...

Okhema

COSMOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

The 'vehicle' or subtle body of the soul in Neoplatonism.

...

Pneuma / Spiritus

COSMOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

The subtle, airy substance that bridges the physical body and the immaterial soul.

...

Sympatheia

COSMOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

The Stoic and Hermetic doctrine of universal sympathy, where all parts of the cosmos are interconnected.

...

LINGUISTIC

Aretalogy

LINGUISTIC · ANALYST TERM

A narrative of the miraculous deeds of a god or hero, common in the Late Antique context of Isis, Asclepius, and Hermes.

...

PHILOSOPHICAL

Archeus

PHILOSOPHICAL

The vital, animating force within living beings.

...

Astral Magic

PHILOSOPHICAL · ACTOR TERM

The practice of drawing down planetary forces into talismans or the human spiritus.

...

Cosmic Ascent

PHILOSOPHICAL

The journey of the soul through the planetary spheres to its divine origin.

...

Doctrine of Correspondence

PHILOSOPHICAL

The axiom 'As above, so below,' linking macrocosm and microcosm.

...

Ennoia (Thought)

PHILOSOPHICAL · ACTOR TERM

The divine thought or conception from which the cosmos emerges.

...

Logos

PHILOSOPHICAL · ACTOR TERM

Word, reason, or discourse. In Hermeticism, the Logos is the creative instrument of the Mind (Nous) and the mediator between the divine and the material

...

Magia Naturalis

PHILOSOPHICAL · ACTOR TERM

Natural magic; the manipulation of occult properties inherently present in nature.

...

Nous

PHILOSOPHICAL · ACTOR TERM

The supreme intellect or divine mind that structures the universe.

...

Philosophical Hermetica

PHILOSOPHICAL · ANALYST TERM

Scholarly category for the theoretical, philosophical, and theological treatises of the Corpus Hermeticum and Asclepius.

...

Phronesis (Practical Wisdom)

PHILOSOPHICAL · ACTOR TERM

A type of wisdom or mindfulness that allows the practitioner to navigate the material world without losing sight of the divine.

...

The Monad

PHILOSOPHICAL

The singular, indivisible principle of unity at the root of all existence.

...

SCIENTIFIC

Technical Hermetica

SCIENTIFIC · ANALYST TERM

Scholarly category for Hermetic texts dealing with practical sciences like alchemy, astrology, and magic.

...

THEOLOGICAL

Christian Kabbalah

THEOLOGICAL · ANALYST TERM

The Renaissance synthesis of Jewish mysticism to prove Christian dogma.

...

Christian Theosophy

THEOLOGICAL · ANALYST TERM

A post-Reformation current originating with Böhme focusing on the inner life of God and nature.

...

Ennead (The Ninth)

THEOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

The ninth sphere, identified with the realm of God or the highest Mind, beyond the Ogdoad.

...

Eusebeia

THEOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

Piety. The proper attitude of the Hermetic practitioner towards God, often identified with the knowledge of God itself.

...

Gnosis

THEOLOGICAL

Experiential knowledge of the divine and the cosmic order.

...

Hermetic Circles

THEOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

The small, informal groups of students and masters in which Hermetic teaching and ritual practice likely took place.

...

Hermetic Persona

THEOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

The idealized figure of Hermes Trismegistus as a sage-prophet, used to authorize Hermetic revelation.

...

Ogdoad (The Eighth)

THEOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

The eighth sphere, above the seven planetary spheres, where the soul begins its final union with the divine after death or during initiation.

...

Philosophia Perennis

THEOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

The idea of a timeless, universal philosophy underlying all religious traditions.

...

Prisca Theologia

THEOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

The doctrine of an ancient, singular theology given by God to early sages.

...

Pronoia (Providence)

THEOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

The divine care or foresight that governs the cosmos, often contrasted with Heimarmene.

...

Serapis

THEOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

A syncretic Greco-Egyptian deity whose cult center (the Serapeum) was a major intellectual hub in Alexandria.

...

The Book of Thoth

THEOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

The legendary Egyptian source text to which many Hermetic works claimed to be related or descended.

...

The Way of Hermes

THEOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

The distinctive Hermetic path of spiritual initiation and contemplation, as described in the Corpus Hermeticum.

...

Theurgy

THEOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

Ritual practices aimed at achieving union with the divine (divine work).

...

Thriskeia

THEOLOGICAL · ACTOR TERM

Cult or Worship. The ritual expression of piety, which in the 'Way of Hermes' often involves internal contemplation and the singing of hymns.

...

Unio Mystica

THEOLOGICAL · ANALYST TERM

The mystical union between the practitioner and the divine Mind, the culmination of the 'Way of Hermes'.

...

Via Universalis

THEOLOGICAL · ANALYST TERM

The concept of a "universal way" of salvation or knowledge that transcends specific cultic boundaries, often associated with Hermeticism and Neoplatonism.
