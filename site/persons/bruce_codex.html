Bruce Codex SCHOLAR

The Bruce Codex (Codex Brucianus) is a Gnostic and Hermetic manuscript discovered in Egypt in 1769, containing the Books of Jeu and other essential texts for understanding the Egyptian-Gnostic-Hermetic milieu.
