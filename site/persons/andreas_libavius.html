Andreas Libavius SCHOLAR

Andreas Libavius (1555–1616) was a German physician and chemist.

Scholarly Significance

Famous for writing Alchemia, often considered the first chemistry textbook. He fiercely opposed Paracelsian mysticism and Rosicrucianism, seeking to strip alchemy of its occult philosophy while preserving its practical laboratory techniques.
