Review: Picatrix -- The Latin Version of the Ghayat al-Hakim (Pingree ed.) SCHOLARSHIP

A scholarly review, published in Nuncius: Annali di Storia della Scienza, of David Pingree's critical edition of the Latin Picatrix (The Warburg Institute, 1986). Compagni's review evaluates Pingree's editorial methodology, his reconstruction of the Latin text from manuscript witnesses, and the editorial apparatus. The review reflects Compagni's expertise in the Latin magical tradition and its relationship to Arabic sources.
